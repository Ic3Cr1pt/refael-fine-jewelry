THE DIAMOND GUIDE

Diamond origins

Natural and lab-grown diamonds share the same crystal structure. Their origins differ in where and how the crystal forms.

Deep in the Earth

Natural diamonds form from carbon deep inside the Earth under intense heat and pressure. After mining, a cutter shapes the facets that direct light through the stone.

In a controlled environment

A lab-grown diamond grows from a tiny seed crystal in a controlled environment. It is then cut and polished, and the quality of the cut affects how much light returns to the eye.
